Rules used in this Decision Tree

  • For this prototype, a slider at 50% or higher counts as Yes.
  • Clean beauty counts as relevant when at least one question is Yes.
  • It counts as one clearly defined category only when retail visibility and brand performance are both Yes.
  • Relevance uses OR: any one Yes makes the answer Yes. Category definition uses AND: retail visibility and brand performance must both be Yes. These are simple Boolean rules.
How this relates to brands

This result describes the category, not an individual brand.

If clean beauty remains relevant but becomes less clearly defined, brands may need to be more specific about what “clean” means for them. The Research Brief explores four possible directions: ingredient safety, proven performance, biotechnology, and environmental and ethical standards.
