The article question

Is Clean Beauty Still Relevant?

Consumers still care about ingredients, safety, responsible production and results. The problem is that “clean beauty” now refers to all of those things at once.

Thesis
Clean beauty is still relevant, but it may no longer work as a single category.
The issue is not whether individual products or brands are credible. It is whether the category still has a clear and useful meaning.
What this tool does

The Research Brief brings together the market context. Move each slider to show what you think will happen, then see how your answers change the overall outlook and what it could mean for brands.

What this could mean for brands

Brands may need to be more specific about what “clean” means for them.

This result describes the category, not an individual brand. The areas below show how brands might remain relevant if the broader clean label becomes less clearly defined. They are possible directions; they do not change the score.

Ingredient safety
What is in the formula, what is left out and how clearly that is explained.
Proven performance
Testing, active ingredients and results the product can demonstrate.
Biotechnology
How new ingredients are developed and made using biological science.
Environmental and ethical standards
How the product is sourced, made, packaged and tested.
